# Daggerheart Campaign Creation

Create a campaign concept collaboratively with a DM.

## When to Use

- Starting a brand new Daggerheart campaign
- DM has no existing campaign documents
- Need to establish world, conflict, and stakes from scratch

## Quick Reference

| Step | Key Question |
|------|--------------|
| Tone | Heroic, gritty, whimsical, or dark? |
| Setting | Classic fantasy, urban, wilderness, unique? |
| Conflict | Who threatens what, and why now? |
| Villain | What do they want? (Not just "evil") |
| Factions | Who are the major players? |

## Process

Work through each step collaboratively. Ask questions, brainstorm together.

### 1. Tone and Theme

Ask about desired tone:
- **Heroic** - Clear heroes vs evil
- **Gritty** - Morally grey, survival
- **Whimsical** - Lighthearted, comedic
- **Dark** - Horror, high stakes, real loss

Also ask about themes: revenge, redemption, found family, power, etc.

### 2. Setting

Ask about the world:
- **Classic fantasy** - Medieval kingdoms, known magic
- **Urban** - City intrigue, factions
- **Wilderness** - Exploration, survival, frontier
- **Unique twist** - What makes this world different?

Use `search_rules` for environment types from the SRD.

### 3. Central Conflict

Brainstorm 2-3 conflict ideas. For each:
- Who/what is the threat?
- What do they want?
- Why now?
- What if heroes fail?

Let DM choose or combine elements.

### 4. Main Villain

Develop the antagonist:
- Name and title
- Motivation (not just evil)
- Methods and resources
- Connection to the world
- Understandable perspective (even if wrong)

Use `list_adversaries` to suggest creature types serving the villain.

### 5. Factions and Locations

Identify 3-5 key elements:
- Major factions and their goals
- Important locations
- How they connect to the conflict

## Output

Save to `campaign/concept.md`:

```markdown
# Campaign Title

**Tone:** 1-2 words
**Setting:** Brief world description

## The Threat
Central conflict summary

## The Villain
Antagonist overview - name, motivation, methods

## Key Factions
- Faction 1: goal
- Faction 2: goal

## Key Locations
- Location 1: significance
- Location 2: significance

## Campaign Hook
How players get involved
```

## Common Mistakes

- **Villain without motive**: "They're evil" isn't a motivation.
- **Static world**: Factions should have goals that advance without players.
- **No player hook**: Ensure clear reason for characters to engage.
